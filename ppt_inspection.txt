  [Text] SLIDE 03  |  SOLUTION ARCHITECTURE & WORKFLOW
  [Text] Closed-Loop Closed-Feedback Precision Soil & Microclimate Management
  [Text] FOUR-STAGE SYSTEM WORKFLOW
  [Text] 1. Edge Sensing Layer: Sub-surface Capacitive Moisture Sensor v1.2, DHT11 Temperature & Relative Humidity, LDR Photodiode, and FR-04 Rain Surface Detector poll every 2000ms.
  [Text] 2. On-Device Agronomy Logic: ESP32 compares volumetric soil water content against crop-specific Field Capacity (FC) and Permanent Wilting Point (PWP) thresholds with built-in hysteresis.
  [Text] 3. Failsafe Actuation Output: Dual optocoupled relays trigger 12V DC irrigation solenoid/pumps and 5V cooling fans. Automatic 15-minute maximum watchdog prevents waterlogging.
  [Text] 4. Web Serial Telemetry Sync: Direct USB-to-Browser connection streams real-time sensor packets at 115,200 baud without installing third-party software or cloud brokers.
  [Text] SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & CLOSED-LOOP FLOW
  [Image] Picture 7 (left=5029200, top=1463040, width=6430975, height=2926080)
  [Text] Edge Autonomy (Zero Internet Required): If internet or Wi-Fi drops, the ESP32 node continues executing its local threshold logic and safety timers autonomously without interruption.
  [Text] Dual Communication Paths: Supports direct Web Serial USB streaming for offline field inspection AND optional REST/WebSocket push when connected to local hotspots.
  [Text] Verified Research & Benchmark Citation: ICAR Precision Farming Development Guidelines & IEEE IoT Standards | Source: https://icar.org.in

--- Slide 5 ---
  [Text] SLIDE 05  |  EMBEDDED FIRMWARE & CONTROL LOGIC
  [Text] Robust C++ Architecture with Active-LOW Safety & Hysteresis Switching
  [Text] FIRMWARE CONTROL ALGORITHM (C++ / ARDUINO)
  [Text] Calibrated ADC Normalization: 12-bit ADC values (0-4095) mapped to volumetric soil moisture percentages using dual-point air/water saturation calibration bounds.
  [Text] Hysteresis Prevention: Irrigation triggers when Soil Moisture < 28% and runs until reaching 45% Field Capacity, preventing rapid relay bouncing and motor burnout.
  [Text] Active-LOW Relay Safety: Relays are initialized to HIGH on startup (pinMode OUTPUT + digitalWrite HIGH) to ensure pumps and fans remain safely OFF during MCU boot.
  [Text] 15-Minute Watchdog Cutoff: Hardware timer enforces a maximum continuous pump run of 15 minutes, safeguarding fields against runaway flooding if a probe is detached.
  [Text] Formatted Serial Telemetry: Outputs structured data packets at 115,200 baud for instantaneous parsing by the browser Web Serial dashboard.
  [Text] HARDWARE PROTOTYPE & SERIAL STREAM
  [Image] Picture 7 (left=6382512, top=1463040, width=5095951, height=2651760)
  [Text] Serial Packet Structure: Temperature (°C), Humidity (%), Soil Moisture (%), Light (%), Rain Status (0/1), Pump Status (ON/OFF), Fan Status (ON/OFF).
  [Text] Non-Blocking millis() Scheduling: Sensor acquisition, relay safety timers, and serial output run on independent non-blocking time intervals without stalling MCU execution.
  [Text] Verified Research & Benchmark Citation: Espressif ESP-IDF Architecture Guidelines & Embedded Systems Safety Standards | Source: https://espressif.com

--- Slide 6 ---
  [Text] SLIDE 06  |  AGRONOMIC INTELLIGENCE & CROP SCIENCE
  [Text] Vapor Pressure Deficit (VPD) Modelling & Soil Moisture Physics
  [Text] AGRONOMY PRINCIPLES & EQUATIONS
  [Text] Soil Water Retention Curve: Tracks Available Water Capacity (AWC) bounded by Field Capacity (~35-45%) and Permanent Wilting Point (~15-18%) for sandy loam soils.
  [Text] Vapor Pressure Deficit (VPD): Calculates atmospheric drying power using Tetens equation: VPD = VPsat * (1 - RH/100). Optimum transpiration zone: 0.8 - 1.2 kPa.
  [Text] Fungal Pathogen Risk Index: Flags mildew and blight risks when relative humidity exceeds 85% at temperatures between 18°C and 26°C for > 6 consecutive hours.
  [Text] Weather Forecast Backoff: Delays automated irrigation cycles if local rainfall probability exceeds 60% within 12 hours, saving reservoir water.
  [Text] SOIL MOISTURE TRANSITION & TRANSPIRATION MATRIX
  [Image] Picture 7 (left=4828032, top=1463040, width=6650431, height=2926080)
  [Text] Dynamic Multi-Crop Profiles: Pre-configured agronomic profiles for Tomatoes, Wheat, Mustard, and Cotton dynamically adjust moisture trigger bands based on crop growth stage.
  [Text] Root-Zone Targeting: Precision drip pulsation allows capillary uptake into root zones without deep drainage losses below root depth.
  [Text] Verified Research & Benchmark Citation: FAO Irrigation and Drainage Paper 56 (Crop Evapotranspiration) & ICAR Agronomy Datasets | Source: https://fao.org
