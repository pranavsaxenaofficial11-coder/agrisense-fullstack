Slide 03 | Solution Architecture & Workflow

Closed-Loop Precision Soil & Microclimate Management

Hardware edge sensing, localized soil physics logic, failsafe relay actuation, and browser telemetry.

🔄 Four-Stage System Workflow
  • 1. Edge Sensing Layer: Sub-surface Capacitive Moisture Sensor v1.2, DHT11 Temperature & RH, LDR Photodiode, and FR-04 Rain Detector poll every 2000ms.
  • 2. On-Device Agronomy Logic: ESP32 compares volumetric soil water content against Field Capacity (FC 35-45%) and Wilting Point (PWP 15-18%) thresholds.
  • 3. Failsafe Actuation Output: Dual optocoupled relays trigger 12V DC irrigation solenoid/pumps. Automatic 15-minute maximum watchdog prevents waterlogging.
  • 4. Web Serial Telemetry Sync: Direct USB-to-Browser connection streams real-time sensor packets at 115,200 baud without installing third-party software.
⚡ Key Engineering Advantages
  • Edge Autonomy (Zero Internet Required): If Wi-Fi drops, the ESP32 node continues executing its local threshold logic and safety timers autonomously.
  • Dual Communication Paths: Supports direct Web Serial USB streaming for offline field inspection AND optional REST/WebSocket push.
  • Hysteresis Switching: Irrigation triggers at < 28% and runs until reaching 45% Field Capacity, preventing rapid relay bouncing and motor burnout.

Slide 05 | Embedded Firmware & Control Logic

Robust C++ Architecture with Active-LOW Safety & Hysteresis

Non-blocking millis() scheduling, dual-point ADC calibration, and automatic 15-minute pump shutoff.

⚙️ Firmware Algorithm Highlights
  • Calibrated ADC Normalization: 12-bit ADC raw values (0-4095) mapped to volumetric moisture using dual-point air/water bounds.
  • Hysteresis Relay Switching: Triggers at < 28% and turns off at 45% Field Capacity to prevent relay bouncing.
  • Active-LOW Relay Safety: Relays initialized to HIGH on startup ensuring pumps remain safely OFF during MCU boot.
  • 15-Minute Watchdog Cutoff: Enforces maximum continuous pump run of 15 mins to safeguard against runaway flooding.
📡 Serial Packet Specification (115,200 Baud)
  • Telemetry Structure: Temp (°C), Humidity (%), Soil Moisture (%), Light (%), Rain Status (0/1), Pump Status (ON/OFF), Fan Status (ON/OFF).
  • Non-Blocking Scheduling: Sensor sampling, relay timers, and serial output execute independently without blocking MCU loops.
  • Driverless Web Serial: Chrome and Edge connect directly without requiring drivers or COM bridges.

Slide 06 | Agronomic Intelligence & Crop Science

Vapor Pressure Deficit (VPD) Modelling & Soil Moisture Physics

Root-zone matric potential, Tetens atmospheric transpiration equation, and fungal pathogen forecasting.

📐 Soil Physics & Mathematical Formulations
  • Soil Water Retention Curve: Tracks Available Water Capacity bounded by Field Capacity (35-45%) and Wilting Point (15-18%).
  • Vapor Pressure Deficit (VPD): Tetens equation: VPD = VPsat * (1 - RH/100). Optimum transpiration zone: 0.8 - 1.2 kPa.
  • Fungal Pathogen Risk Index: Flags blight risks when RH > 85% at temperatures between 18°C and 26°C for > 6 hours.
  • Weather Forecast Backoff: Delays irrigation if rain probability > 60% within 12 hours.
🌿 Dynamic Multi-Crop Profiles
  • Tomatoes (HY-203): Target 30-42% moisture | Vegetative to Fruiting stage.
  • Wheat (Rabi): Target 28-38% moisture | Tillering to Grain filling stage.
  • Mustard & Cotton: Calibrated moisture depletion curves for sandy loam soils.
  • Capillary Uptake: Precision drip pulsation prevents deep drainage leaching below root zones.
