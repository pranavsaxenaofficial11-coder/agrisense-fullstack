Your privacy & data

Before you start, here's exactly what AgriSense uses and why. We keep it minimal.

👤
Account info — your name & email, to sign you in and sync your data. Stored securely in Firebase.
📍
Location — only when you tap “My location” on the map, to centre the satellite view. Never tracked in the background.
🌦️
Region & weather — the area you set, sent to Open-Meteo for live forecasts.
🧠
AI features — questions and photos you submit go to our AI provider (OpenRouter) to generate answers. Don’t upload anything private.
📊
Anonymous analytics — privacy-friendly visit counts via Cloudflare. No personal profiling, no ad tracking.

We never sell your data. This is a student project — some features (payments, delivery) are simulated. By continuing you agree to this data use.

You can still browse, but some features (login, AI, location) need this consent to work.
